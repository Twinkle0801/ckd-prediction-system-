# Chronic Kidney Disease (CKD)

## What is CKD?

Chronic Kidney Disease (CKD) is a long-term condition in which the kidneys gradually lose their ability to filter waste products and excess fluid from the blood.

CKD is usually diagnosed using:

- eGFR
- Urine Albumin
- Serum Creatinine

Common causes include:

- Diabetes
- Hypertension

Symptoms may include:

- Fatigue
- Swelling
- Changes in urination

Treatment depends on the stage of disease.
